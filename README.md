# AFE2 Build Editor

Plan your next **Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2** loadout in your browser. Choose a
class, experiment with perks, and put together your weapons and equipment
before your next mission.

The goal is simple: make builds easier to understand and fun to experiment
with, whether you’re learning the basics or fine-tuning a favourite setup.
The editor uses data from the game for its choices, descriptions, and perk
connections.

## Get started

**A hosted version is planned.** The link will appear here when it’s ready.

Once you’re in the editor:

1. Choose your class and abilities.
2. Arrange perks on the grid and explore how they connect.
3. Pick your weapons, attachments, and mission equipment.

Your current build saves automatically in your browser, ready for your next
visit.
