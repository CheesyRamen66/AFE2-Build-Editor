# AFE2 Build Editor

AFE2 Build Editor is a browser tool for planning your loadouts in
**Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2**. Bring your class, abilities, perks, weapons, and
equipment together in one place, and work out what you want to take into your
next mission.

## Why this project exists

Putting together a build is part of the fun. The aim here is to give players
room to explore: learn a new class, find perks that support a favourite
ability, or see how a different weapon fits into a loadout. It should be easy
to get started and useful when you want to spend an afternoon getting every
piece into place.

The project is built around making those choices clear. Descriptions help
you explore what each option offers, the perk grid helps you understand what
connects, and equipment pickers show compatible choices. The underlying
catalogue comes from the game’s own data, giving the editor a foundation that
can be refreshed as the game changes.

## Put together your first build

When you open the editor, start with a class you enjoy or one you want to
learn:

1. **Choose your class and abilities.** Select a class, then click its ability
   slots to explore the available choices. These give you a starting point
   for the rest of your build.
2. **Build out your perk grid.** Search for perks by name or by an effect in
   their description, such as reload speed. Click or drag perks onto the
   board, then rotate and rearrange them to fit. Connection indicators help
   you spot modifiers that still need a compatible connection.
3. **Make your weapons your own.** Pick weapons for your class and explore
   their components, traits, and augments. Each slot shows the options that
   fit it.
4. **Finish with mission equipment.** Choose your Major and Minor items,
   then revisit any part of the loadout as your idea takes shape.

For a useful shortcut, hover over a perk or ability on the grid and press
**R** to browse compatible modifiers. Press **D** to rotate a perk you’re
placing.

Your current build is saved automatically in the same browser. For now, the
editor keeps one draft; choosing another class starts a fresh build.

## Where it’s heading

The editor is still in development, and a public website is planned. A link
will be added here when it’s available. Further ahead, the goal is to let
players share builds and browse community creations, so the project becomes
a place to learn from each other as well as experiment.

## Help shape it

Feedback from players is welcome. If something is confusing, a description
looks wrong, or an option seems to be missing,
[open an issue](https://github.com/CheesyRamen66/AFE2-Build-Editor/issues).
Include the class, perk, or equipment involved and what you expected to happen.
Ideas and code contributions are welcome too.

To try the editor locally or contribute, start with the
[local setup instructions](TECHNICAL.md#run-the-local-editor). The
[catalogue guide](RUNBOOK.md) covers preparing the game data, and the
[technical reference](TECHNICAL.md) explains how the project works.
